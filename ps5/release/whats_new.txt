01.002.000
- Port menu: press Touchpad + Options during the game. All of the port's settings are in one place now, and the game's own Options are as on the Xbox.
- Sharper 3D geometry in 4K. Geometry Precision brings back the original 640×480 grid.
- Cars, traffic and scenery switch to simpler models up to 8 times farther away (Detail Distance).
- Motion Blur: Off, Low or Original.
- Performance overlay and a 60-second benchmark.
- Fixed coloured blocks over races at a low Resolution Scale.
01.001.000
- 5.1 surround. Cars and effects keep the game's own 5.1 positions on a surround system, through the PS5's native audio output. The PS5 follows your speaker setup and folds it down for TV speakers and headphones; stereo still works.
- Multichannel Sound plays music and the front pair from every speaker too (on by default), and Speaker Test plays a tone from one speaker at a time, or from all of them in turn, to check the layout.
- Quitting to create an online account closes the app to the home screen instead of a crash report.
